Private Property app

Register as a driver.

Use this form to register for the Hooked416 Private Property app — the app our drivers use on private-property tows. A manager reviews every registration before it is approved. Once you are approved, we email you a one-time link to set your own PIN (a short personal code), and you sign in to the app with your phone number and that PIN.

Registering does not guarantee approval. Your information goes to the Hooked416 office only.

What you need.

Have your MTO tow driver licence ready. The form asks for the TD number printed on that licence, and a photo or PDF copy of the licence itself.

Questions? Call the office at 416-301-0820.
