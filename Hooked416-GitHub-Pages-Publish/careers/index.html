Intact Driver Profile Survey

1. Download the blank Intact survey (.doc) ↓

2. Open it in Word or a compatible app. Complete all applicable driver sections, review the authorization and sign where indicated. Leave insured/company signatures for Hooked416.

3. Save the completed form as PDF, DOC or DOCX, then attach it here. If completing it on paper, scan all pages into one PDF.

PDF, DOC or DOCX · maximum 5 MB. All attachments together, including any tow certificate, must be no larger than 10 MB.

PDF only · maximum 5 MB per file, 10 MB total for all attachments, including any tow certificate. Scan paper records into one PDF.
Need a separate licence history for insurance?

A driver’s licence history is different from a 3-year driver record: it does not include convictions or demerit points. Only order this additional document if Hooked416 or the insurer requests it; it does not replace the required driver’s abstract.

Driver’s licence photo

Upload one clear colour photo of the front of your current driver’s licence. Place it flat, avoid glare and include all four corners so the details are readable. Use this dedicated field; do not put licence details in the questionnaire.

JPG, PNG or HEIC/HEIF · maximum 5 MB. On a phone, choose a photo from your photo library or Files. All attachments together, including any tow certificate, must total 10 MB or less.

Your licence photo contains personal information and will be sent through FormSubmit to hooked416@gmail.com with your application for driver identification and insurance review. It will not appear publicly on this website.
